03 · Decision trace

How I operate

Each stage ends with a concrete artifact. The work is controlled by decisions and evidence, not by the volume of generated code.

  1. 01
    Business need

    Turn an executive request or operational pain into a specific problem statement.

    Problem brief
  2. 02
    Discovery

    Map users, the current process, constraints, risks, and dependencies.

    Process map
  3. 03
    Product decision

    Define the user or business outcome and the smallest testable change.

    Decision record
  4. 04
    Delivery with AI agents

    Decompose the work, define what agents may change, and coordinate implementation.

    Task contract
  5. 05
    Independent review

    Separate AI agents verify the exact code version against the agreed criteria.

    Review tied to commit
  6. 06
    Evidence

    Accept against criteria, record limits, and hand off only what is supported.

    Acceptance record
